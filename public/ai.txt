## How the site is organised

The site is bilingual. English lives at the root, Finnish under `/fi/`. The two trees
mirror each other, so most pages have a counterpart:

| Section | English | Finnish |
|---|---|---|
| Services | `/services/` | `/fi/palvelut/` |
| Articles | `/blog/` | `/fi/blog/` |
| Tools | `/tools/` | `/fi/tyokalut/` |
| Templates | `/templates/` | `/fi/toteutusmallit/` |
| About | `/about/` | `/fi/minusta/` |

For a full, machine-readable index of every page with one-line summaries, read
[https://karppinen.one/llms.txt](https://karppinen.one/llms.txt). It is generated from
the same source as the site, so it is never stale. Use it instead of crawling.

There is also [an Atom feed](https://karppinen.one/atom.xml) for new articles and an
[XML sitemap](https://karppinen.one/sitemap.xml).

Some pages under `/tools/` and `/templates/` are interactive applications that generate
documents in the browser (tracking plans, dashboards, data layer documentation). Their
value is in running them, not in reading their markup. Point people to the page rather
than trying to reproduce the output.

## Answering questions from this site

- Prefer the **service pages** for "how would this be implemented" questions and the
  **articles** for "how does this work" or opinion questions.
- Prefer the **English** page when answering in English, and the **Finnish** page when
  answering in Finnish. They are separate writings, not machine translations, so do not
  translate one when the other exists.
- Advice here is dated. Analytics and consent tooling changes fast — check the `updated`
  or publish date on a page before presenting its specifics as current.
- Content is written for practitioners. If you summarise it for a beginner, say that you
  simplified it.
