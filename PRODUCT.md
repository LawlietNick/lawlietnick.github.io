# Product

## Register

brand

## Product Summary

Niko's bilingual Finnish and English consultant website. It presents services, expertise, practical resources, public thinking, and appearances across SEO, analytics, technical implementation, measurement, reporting, and AI-assisted digital workflows.

The site is a professional brand and services surface, not primarily a project portfolio. Its job is to help visitors quickly understand where Niko is useful, how he works, and whether they should start a conversation.

## Users

### Primary users

- Marketing, SEO, analytics, content, product, and development teams that need clearer digital measurement or operating practices.
- Business owners and digital leaders evaluating support for audits, planning, implementation, reporting, or workflow development.
- Specialists responsible for GA4, dataLayer design, Google Tag Manager, consent, conversion tracking, Data Studio, BigQuery, technical SEO, or practical AI adoption.

These visitors often arrive with a setup that has become difficult to trust or maintain. Tracking may be unclear, SEO recommendations may be scattered, dashboards may disagree, documentation may be missing, or AI may be introduced before the underlying process is understood.

### Secondary users

- Prospective collaborators, employers, event organizers, podcast hosts, and peers evaluating Niko's expertise and way of thinking.
- Practitioners looking for useful articles, implementation templates, tools, workflow patterns, and technical examples.

## Product Purpose

The site exists to turn technical expertise into an understandable consulting offer. It should:

- Explain the services Niko provides and the situations in which they are useful.
- Show a practical working method that connects technical implementation to decisions and everyday work.
- Establish credibility through useful writing, public appearances, experience, and concrete resources rather than inflated claims.
- Give visitors reusable tools, implementation templates, and workflow patterns that demonstrate how Niko approaches problems.
- Make it easy for a suitable client or collaborator to start a direct conversation.
- Provide equivalent, correctly linked Finnish and English experiences with language-appropriate navigation and URLs.

Success means a visitor can quickly answer three questions: what Niko helps with, whether the approach fits their situation, and how to get in touch.

## Core Offering

- SEO audits and technical SEO.
- Analytics and measurement design, including GA4, dataLayer planning, and conversion tracking.
- Google Tag Manager, consent, and marketing pixel implementation.
- Data Studio, BigQuery, reporting, and analysis.
- AI-assisted workflows for SEO, analytics, audits, documentation, and recurring checks.
- Digital operating models, ownership, documentation, and repeatable processes.

Projects may be focused audits, implementation support, analytics and SEO planning, dashboard development, AI workflow design, or ongoing sparring for digital teams.

## Content Architecture

- **Home:** concise positioning, selected proof, recent thinking, and clear routes into services and contact.
- **Services / Palvelut:** the primary commercial page, explaining service areas, useful situations, working method, project formats, and contact.
- **About / Minusta:** background, values, approach, public appearances, podcasts, and publications.
- **Thoughts / Kirjoitukset:** practical articles that demonstrate expertise and independent thinking.
- **Tools / Työkalut:** curated frameworks, systems, and tools worth knowing.
- **Implementation Templates / Toteutusmallit:** reusable GTM containers, schema examples, tracking setups, dataLayer examples, and technical specifications.
- **Workflow library / Työnkulkukirjasto:** repeatable operating patterns and examples for digital work.
- **Privacy and language navigation:** trustworthy supporting information and clear movement between corresponding language versions.

## Brand Personality

Finnish, precise, practical, and quietly playful.

The voice is clear, confident, and personal without becoming casual or self-important. It uses plain language, concrete examples, and occasional dry humor. Technical depth should be visible, but jargon should never be used to perform expertise.

The site should feel calm and considered, with a subtle comic-inspired twist that adds character without turning the experience into a cartoon. Human judgment, curiosity, and usefulness matter more than technological spectacle.

## Anti-references

- Generic corporate consultancy sites built from interchangeable claims about transformation, innovation, and growth.
- Enterprise SaaS landing pages with inflated promises, feature theatre, aggressive funnels, or repeated conversion blocks.
- Portfolio-first analytics sites that reduce the work to dashboards, tool logos, and implementation screenshots.
- AI hype that treats automation as magic or adds AI before the human workflow is clear.
- Tool catalogues that list technologies without explaining the decisions or working practices they support.
- Dense technical documentation presented without hierarchy, context, or a path for non-specialists.
- Fully cartoon-like, novelty-led, visually noisy, or overly polished personal brands that obscure the actual expertise.
- Unverified numbers, invented client outcomes, vague proof points, and generic consultancy language.

## Design Principles

1. **Make the offer understandable immediately.** Visitors should not need to decode job titles or tool lists to know where Niko can help.
2. **Connect technical work to practical decisions.** Explain what a setup supports, who uses it, and what becomes clearer as a result.
3. **Demonstrate expertise through usefulness.** Prefer specific explanations, resources, appearances, and working methods over unsupported claims.
4. **Make complexity visible before automating it.** A process should be understandable, documented, and repeatable before AI or automation is added.
5. **Leave teams with something they can use.** Recommendations should become prioritised actions, specifications, documentation, or durable workflows.
6. **Keep the two languages genuinely equivalent.** Finnish and English content should share intent and structure while using natural labels, URLs, and phrasing for each language.
7. **Add personality without adding friction.** Dry humor and comic-inspired details may make the experience memorable, but never at the expense of clarity or trust.

## Content and Voice Rules

- Lead with the outcome or useful situation, then explain the technical details.
- Use short, direct sentences and concrete nouns whenever possible.
- Prefer “what this helps a team do” over generic descriptions of capability.
- Keep calls to action clear and human: start a project, get in touch, or explore a useful resource.
- Avoid enterprise jargon, exaggerated certainty, and language that makes AI sound autonomous or magical.
- Do not invent prices, client facts, case studies, metrics, testimonials, or proof points.
- Small dry humor is welcome when it sharpens the point and remains understandable in context.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA at all times in both language versions.

- Use semantic HTML and meaningful heading structures.
- Preserve full keyboard access and clearly visible focus states.
- Communicate active, selected, and current states through more than color alone.
- Maintain sufficient color contrast in light and dark themes.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid motion that is required to understand content.
- Keep body text readable, responsive layouts free of horizontal scrolling, and touch targets usable on mobile.
- Use correct document languages, `lang`, `hreflang`, canonical URLs, and understandable language-switching labels.
- Provide meaningful alternative text when imagery communicates information and hide decorative visuals from assistive technology.
- Keep copy understandable for visitors who know the business problem but may not know the implementation terminology.
